The Soul Of Dining

Where Ancient Charcoal Meets Pure Culinary Poetry.

Roohani (meaning spiritual or of the soul) was conceived to restore the lost majesty of slow-charcoal cooking, gentle saffron infusions, and royal court delicacies to modern Dhaka.

Located conveniently in Metropolitan Shopping Plaza, Gulshan, we source prime artisan cuts, ground hand-roasted spices daily, and let marinades breathe overnight in unglazed clay. Every plate tells an intimate story of indulgence, balance, and quiet grandeur.
